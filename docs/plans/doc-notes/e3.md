## User page: usage/Split-Editor.md (outline)

1. What it is
   - The editor area can hold two groups of tabs side by side, like VS Code's editor groups. Each group has its own tab strip and its own tab on screen.
   - The left group also shows the Diff tab (a change picked in Changes) and the Log. The right group shows tabs only.
   - At most two groups.
2. Split the editor
   - Window > Split Right (Cmd+\, Ctrl+\ elsewhere): the tab on screen also opens in a right group, which takes the keyboard.
   - Right-click a tab in the left group > Split Right (file tabs only).
   - Quick Open (Cmd+P): Cmd+Enter on a file opens it to the side (the other group). Enter opens it in the focused group.
   - Files panel: right-click a file > Open to the Side.
   - Focus Right Group (Cmd+2) with one group splits too, as in VS Code.
3. The same file in both groups
   - It is one document: typing in one shows in the other at once. Unsaved state (the dot on the tab) is the same in both; Save (Cmd+S) in either writes the file once.
   - Each side keeps its own caret, scroll and undo: Cmd+Z in a group undoes only what you typed there.
   - Closing the file in one group keeps your unsaved edits: the other group still has them, so no question is asked. Closing the last one asks as usual.
4. Moving around
   - Click anywhere in a group to focus it; the focused group's tab has the accent line, the other group's tab on screen is dimmed.
   - Window > Focus Left Group (Cmd+1) and Focus Right Group (Cmd+2) put the keyboard there.
   - Next Tab / Previous Tab (Shift+Cmd+] / [) go round the focused group's strip.
   - Cmd+W closes the focused group's tab on screen.
   - The status bar, breadcrumbs, Back and Forward, Quick Open ":" and "@", the Git > Current File items, blame and the Search Everywhere text from a selection all follow the focused group.
5. Moving tabs and closing groups
   - Right-click a tab > Move to Right Group / Move to Left Group, or Window > Move Tab to Other Group. Unsaved edits move with the tab.
   - Commit, history, branches and terminal tabs live in one group at a time: Split Right and Open to the Side move them instead of copying.
   - A group closes when its last tab closes (the left group stays while it shows the Diff tab or the Log). The other group then takes the whole width.
   - Window > Close Group, or right-click a tab > Close Group, closes the focused group with its tabs (asks about unsaved edits not open in the other group).
   - Close Others, Close to the Right and Close All in a tab's menu act on that group only.
6. Size
   - Drag the bar between the groups; double-click it to share the width equally. The size is remembered (state.json `editorSplitRatio`, 20% to 80%).
7. Tabs and the tab limit
   - Reopen tabs on start brings both groups back, with the focused one.
   - Reopen Closed Tab (Shift+Cmd+T) puts a tab back in the group it closed in, if that group is still there.
   - The tab limit counts every tab in both groups; a group's tab on screen is never closed by it, so single tab mode keeps one tab per group.
8. Turn it off
   - Settings > Editor > Tabs > Split editor (on by default). Off, every tab moves into one group and the group commands are disabled ("Split editor is off in Settings").

## Developer chapter: developer/How-the-Split-Editor-Works.md (outline)

1. Why we need it: compare two files, or two places of one file, without switching tabs; matches VS Code.
2. The pieces and where they live
   - `src/lib/stores/editorGroups.ts` (pure, tested in `editorGroups.test.ts`): `GroupsState { groups, focused, nextId }`, at most `MAX_GROUPS` (2). Functions: openTarget, openInGroup, closeInGroups, closedBetween, dropEmptyGroups, splitRight, moveToOtherGroup, closeGroup, mergeGroups, groupEvictions (tab limit), restoredGroups, sideOf.
   - `src/lib/stores/repo.svelte.ts`: holds `groupsState`; `tabs` (every tab once) and `openFilePath` (the focused group's tab) are `$derived` from it, so old callers keep working. `view` is now a getter and setter over the first group's view; setting "diff" or "log" focuses the first group. Group-aware methods: activateTab, closeTabs(paths, groupId | null), focusGroup, focusGroupAt, splitRight, moveTabToOtherGroup, closeGroup, mergeGroups, tidyGroups, targetGroupFor.
   - `src/lib/views/changes/selection.svelte.ts`: `primaryView` (what the first group shows), `shownView` (the focused group's view), `logShown`, `tabShown(groupId, tabPath)`, `tabOnScreen(tabPath)`.
   - `src/lib/editor/sharedDocs.ts` (tested in `sharedDocs.test.ts`): replaySpec, isReplay, the SharedDocs registry and syncedDispatch.
   - `src/lib/views/Workspace.svelte`: one `<section class="editor-group">` per group (keyed by group id), a ResizeHandle between them; `focusin`/`pointerdown` focus the group. `EditorTabs.svelte` takes a `groupId`. `FileView.svelte` takes a `groupId`.
   - `src/lib/stores/fileCommands.svelte.ts`: a list of editors per path; the one in the focused group answers (`preferred`).
   - `src/lib/stores/navigation.svelte.ts`: reveal requests carry a `groupId`; `focusEditor` asks a not yet mounted editor to take the keyboard.
   - Menus: `menuIds.ts`, `menuSpec.ts` (Window menu), `menuState.ts` (`editorGroups` input), `menuActions.ts`, `registry.ts` (disabled reasons), `workspaceActions.ts` (splitEditorRight, moveEditorTab).
3. Mermaid ideas
   - flowchart: Quick Open Cmd+Enter / Files panel / Window menu -> targetGroupFor -> openInGroup -> dropEmptyGroups -> applyGroups (closed tabs remembered, tabsClosed listeners, session save).
   - sequenceDiagram: typing in the left view -> dispatchTransactions -> view.update -> replaySpec -> right view.dispatch (replayedChange, addToHistory false) -> right listeners skip dirty, auto save and history -> left listener calls setDirty -> both tabs show the dot.
   - stateDiagram: one group -> Split Right -> two groups -> last tab of a group closes -> one group (the right one takes the left place, ids stay).
4. Shared documents
   - Two EditorViews with their own states, synced like CodeMirror's split view example. A replay has no user event, so it does not open completions on the other side.
   - SharedDoc per path: baseline (saved text), diskVersion, saving, members. Dirty is computed by the editor that made the change against the shared baseline; Save writes once (shared saving flag); only the first editor (leader) does quiet reloads from disk, the others refresh their marks and blame.
   - A second editor starts from the first one's text (unsaved edits included), not from disk.
   - Moving a dirty tab destroys its editor and creates another: the leaving editor leaves its text in the SharedDoc, and clean-up waits one task (`setTimeout(0)`) so the new editor, which joins in the same Svelte update, starts from it.
5. Design decisions
   - Two groups at most; stable ids so the right group's editors stay mounted when the left one closes.
   - Only file tabs can be in both groups (a terminal's DOM node is moved, not copied; commit and Git tabs hold live state).
   - The first group keeps the Diff tab and the Log: they are not tabs, and one place for them keeps the Changes and Log flows unchanged.
   - Plain opens show a tab where it is already open (focused group first), else in the focused group.
   - One tab limit for both groups, never closing a group's visible tab.
   - Tab session: `tabs`/`active` stay the left group (old state.json files load as before), with optional `right` and `rightFocused`; an empty right group means no split.
   - Closed tab stack remembers `side` (0 or 1).
   - Dormant tabs are keyed by group and path.
   - Shortcuts as VS Code: Cmd+\ (the panel terminal keeps Cmd+\ for its own split while focused, since it handles the key first), Cmd+1, Cmd+2.
   - No tab dragging between strips yet: there is no tab drag code, and Tauri's drag and drop handling makes HTML5 drag unreliable on Windows.
6. Tests: editorGroups.test.ts (21), sharedDocs.test.ts (7), tabSession.test.ts (split sessions), settingsData.test.ts (split editor settings).
7. Keeping in sync: a new kind of pseudo tab needs nothing here (pseudoTabs.ts decides); new tab commands take a groupId; anything that reads "the tab on screen" should use `changesSelection.tabShown` or the focused group, not `tabs`.

## Settings and shortcuts lines

- Settings page (Editor > Tabs): "Split editor: Show two groups of tabs side by side with Window > Split Right. Turning it off moves every tab into one group." Default on. settings.json key `splitEditor`.
- state.json: `editorSplitRatio` (0.2 to 0.8, default 0.5); `openTabs[workspace].right` and `.rightFocused`.
- Keyboard shortcuts page (Window menu): Split Right Cmd+\ (Ctrl+\), Focus Left Group Cmd+1 (Ctrl+1), Focus Right Group Cmd+2 (Ctrl+2); Move Tab to Other Group and Close Group have no key. All can get custom keys and appear in the Command Palette.
- Help > Keyboard Shortcuts, Quick Open section: "Open to the side: Cmd+Enter, on a file in Quick Open, with the split editor on".
- Menus page: Window menu gains a group block after Pin Tab.
- Editor-and-Tabs page: link to Split-Editor.md; tab menu gains Split Right, Move to Right/Left Group, Close Group.
- Files-Panel page: file menu gains Open to the Side.
- Navigation and Quick Open pages: Cmd+Enter opens to the side; Back/Forward follow the focused group.
