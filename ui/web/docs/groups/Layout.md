# Layout components

Generated by `bun run ui:docs`. Back to the [index](../../COMPONENTS.md).

- [Stack](../components/Stack.md): Lays out children in a row or a column with a gap from the spacing tokens.
- [Text](../components/Text.md): Text in a named text style from the design tokens, so words never need a class or
  color.
- [Grid](../components/Grid.md): A grid of equal columns: a fixed count, or as many as fit at a minimum width.
- [Card](../components/Card.md): A bordered box that groups rows, like a worktree card in the AI panel.
- [Divider](../components/Divider.md): A 1px line between groups: horizontal by default, vertical in toolbars.
- [Row](../components/Row.md): One row of a list: hover, selected color, fold chevron, indent and hover actions around
  your content.
- [Spacer](../components/Spacer.md): Takes the free room in a row, so what comes after it sits at the far end.
- [Time](../components/Time.md): A moment relative to now, kept current: 5 min ago, 5m, or in 3h 20m, with the full date
  as tooltip.
- [SplitPane](../components/SplitPane.md): Two panes side by side or stacked, with a ResizeHandle between; `size` is the
  first pane's px.
- [ResizeHandle](../components/ResizeHandle.md): A drag handle on a panel's edge: a line that becomes an accent bar on
  hover; keys and double-click.
- [StatusBar](../components/StatusBar.md): The bottom status bar: StatusItems on the left (repository, branch) and on
  the right.
- [StatusItem](../components/StatusItem.md): One status bar item: an icon and short text; a button when it has
  `onclick`.
- [ActivityBar](../components/ActivityBar.md): The vertical icon bar beside a side panel; the open panel is tinted with
  an accent line.
- [PanelHeader](../components/PanelHeader.md): A panel's header: its title in small capitals on the left, its action
  buttons on the right.
- [DescriptionList](../components/DescriptionList.md): Label and value pairs in two columns, like a commit's Author,
  Date and Parents.
- [AppShell](../components/AppShell.md): The window layout: header, activity bars, resizable side panels, editor area,
  bottom panel, status bar.
- [Toolbar](../components/Toolbar.md): A row of controls along a view's edge: a tab's top bar, a panel's bar, the
  editors' bars, footers.
- [SectionTitle](../components/SectionTitle.md): A small heading over a group of rows, with a count and buttons at its
  end.
- [AppHeader](../components/AppHeader.md): The bar across the top of the window: navigation and pickers on the left,
  busy work and actions right.
- [TreeLine](../components/TreeLine.md): The connector in front of a grouped row, like split terminals in the terminal
  list.
- [ListBox](../components/ListBox.md): A bordered, scrolling box on the editor background holding a list inside a
  dialog.
- [Pane](../components/Pane.md): A side column inside a view or dialog: a set width, its own scroll and a tinted
  background.
- [ElementHost](../components/ElementHost.md): A box whose element the app fills itself, such as a terminal that moves
  between places.
- [SplitGrid](../components/SplitGrid.md): The editor area split into groups placed by fractions, with lines (or rounded
  gaps) between them.
- [PaneRow](../components/PaneRow.md): Views side by side in one panel, like split terminals: shares of the width, lines
  between, focus line.
- [SplitHandle](../components/SplitHandle.md): A handle between two side-by-side views in one box: drag or arrow keys
  move the split.
